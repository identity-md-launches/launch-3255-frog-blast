Frog Blast

A garden bomb arena. Last frog standing wins the round; first to three wins the match.

Bot difficulty

You against three bots. Keyboard or touch.

Two players sharing one keyboard. Not available on touch screens.

    How to play

    Controls

    Player 1
    WASD move, Space drops a bomb
    Player 2
    Arrow keys move, Enter drops a bomb
    Touch
    Solo only: d-pad on the left, bomb button on the right
    Pause
    P or Esc, or the pause button

    Bombs are dangerous to everyone

    • A bomb bursts after 2.5 seconds. Its blast runs in four straight lines and hurts any frog in it, including you.
    • Step off your bomb right away. You cannot walk back onto it.
    • Stone stops a blast. The first crate in a line breaks and stops it too.
    • A blast that reaches another bomb sets it off at once.
    • Crates can hide upgrades: extra bomb, longer blast and faster hops.

    Winning

    • One life per round, 2 minutes per round. Last frog standing wins it.
    • If the last frogs fall together or time runs out, the round is a draw.
    • First to three round wins takes the match. Score: 100 per rival out, 10 per crate, 25 per round won.
